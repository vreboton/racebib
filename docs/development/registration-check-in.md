# Runner verification and check-in

Open **Check-in** within **Runner Registrations**. Search by bib, first name, last name, or full name. Search prioritizes an exact bib match and shows the information needed to verify the runner at the desk.

A confirmed registration with an assigned bib and an active race can check in during **Registration Open**, **Registration Closed**, **Ready**, or **Live**. Closing registration is optional. Reversal is available on checked-in registrations in all event stages except Completed and Cancelled. Supply a short operational reason without runner contact or sensitive details. The audit history records both operations and their actors, plus the reversal reason.

While checked in, a runner's race, category, wave, bib and registration status are fixed. Reverse check-in before changing any of them. Name on Bib, team, and payment corrections remain available under existing validation. Check-in and reversal leave registration and payment statuses as they were.

Race Directors and Event Administrators use their existing organizer permissions. Event-scoped check-in permissions are future work before expanding access to volunteers.
